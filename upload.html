Format spec

Papers are written against a fixed template — the parser accepts that documented format, not arbitrary LaTeX. The templates define every macro it understands, and templates/README.md explains each one plus the conventions that are easy to get wrong (how mark-scheme labels carry across rows, and the two different mstab column counts).
